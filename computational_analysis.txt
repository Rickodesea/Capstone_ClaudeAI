  Real-Time MILP
  - The LP relaxation (GLOP) scales predictably: J=16→1024 at N=1024 goes 314ms→15.7s, roughly O(J × N) — this matches the LP complexity of a J×N variable problem.
  - The exact MILP (CBC) is fragile under high load: J=64 on N=16 nodes (4 jobs/node) hit the 15-minute cap while J=16 on N=64 (0.25 jobs/node) solved in 132ms with identical variable
  count (1,024). The difficulty is not variable count alone — it is problem tightness (jobs competing for scarce machines makes the branch-and-bound tree exponentially deeper).
  - Production viability: realistic per-group sizes (J=5–20, N=5–50) consistently solve in < 200ms. The model is well within real-time constraints for its intended use.

  Plan-Ahead MISOCP
  - The model solved cleanly up to ~477k variables (T=256, N=256, 756s), with MIP gap < 0.01% in every solved case — solution quality is excellent.
  - Hard memory wall hit at ~535k variables (T=128, N=512 and larger): Gurobi ran out of RAM before the time limit. This is the binding constraint, not solve time.
  - The practical boundary is approximately T ≤ 128, N ≤ 256 (≈242k vars) for reliable solves. Beyond that, decomposition (Benders, column generation, or rolling horizon) is required — the
  monolithic MISOCP is not scalable to production-scale cluster sizes (T=90, N=250 is already at the edge).
  - The single capped case (T=512, N=512, 1.9M vars, ~40min estimate) confirms the limitation shown earlier: this problem class needs structural decomposition to be operationally viable.
